# GSE174554 expression validation report

This report summarizes the target-sealed source-expression validation used in the manuscript.

## Cohort and target access

- Thirty physical patients were frozen by the eligibility procedure before confirmation scoring.
- Four prespecified integrity failures were retained without replacement or repartitioning.
- The analyzed split contained 16 development and 10 confirmation patients.
- Recurrence expression was not used for predictor construction.
- Source pseudobulk expression was generated by summing raw counts within patient, aligning 33,694 genes, converting to CPM and applying `log1p`.
- Target composition used the study authors' Tumor/Normal labels only.

## Leakage and integrity checks

- All included source and target compositions passed simplex checks.
- Twenty-one target-access and leakage checks passed with no failures.
- Candidate construction, feature selection, scaling, reference fitting and deployment-weight selection used development data only.
- Confirmation targets were exposed only for scoring after prediction artifacts were fixed.

## Primary Honest Confirmation result

Five prespecified candidates completed the two-state analysis. ExpressionElasticNet reduced mean confirmation MAE relative to MeanDelta but did not obtain finite-sample support for Utility, and development-only deployment-weight selection chose zero candidate influence. No candidate qualified after candidate-family correction.

The conclusion is an evidence statement about retained predictive influence under the frozen contract. It is not a claim about causal structure, individual-patient safety or clinical benefit.

## Secondary diagnostics

- Dependent LOPO was completed for 26 patients and is reported only as a diagnostic.
- Bootstrap-size and seed checks were completed for the prespecified candidate subset.
- Fixed-prediction reference and loss sensitivity analyses were completed without replacing the primary contract.
- Full-pipeline delete-one analyses completed for ExpressionElasticNet and composition OT.
- Tail-harm summaries and deterministic reruns are included in the machine-readable outputs.

## Reproducibility

The packaged extension passes 136 regression tests. The `audit/` directory records input hashes, patient eligibility, target-access checks, frozen patient partitions and claim-to-output traceability. The `source_data/` directory contains the machine-readable tables used for the expression-validation figure and sensitivity analyses. The processed primary-source expression object required by the executed analysis is included; recurrence expression is not represented as a predictor input.
